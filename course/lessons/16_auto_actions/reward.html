Keep both CAP lines exactly as Path Capture wrote them (your seq number may differ).

Make it yours
  • Change the timing by changing the lap counts: laps = seconds × 1000 ÷ 20. For example, 4 seconds of shooting is 200 laps.
  • Mind the clock: as written, the shot takes about 8 of AUTO's 30 seconds.
  • Using a motor-driven Feeder instead of the indexer? Use robot.feeder.feed() and robot.feeder.stop().
